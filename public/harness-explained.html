You describe the change. The AI works on a draft. The harness coordinates the tools, checks the result, and controls when it reaches your canvas.

What is a harness?

Think of it as the workbench and quality checks around the AI. The AI decides what to propose; the harness gives it a controlled way to read, edit, review, and hand over the work.

Start with the real canvas.

The AI reads the latest workflow and your selection. It uses your request and recent conversation to identify the change, rather than assuming an earlier version is still current.

A second look can take three paths.

The request is satisfied

Let the change through.

The checks accept the draft. The canvas gets the reviewed changes and you get a concise explanation.

→ Review → apply
The AI made a mistake

Send it back for repair.

If the draft misses an explicit requirement, the AI gets up to two review-driven repair attempts. Each repair goes through arrangement, validation, and review again.

↶ Review → draft → checks again
A tradeoff or unknown appears

Bring you into the decision.

A design concern can accompany a valid requested edit. The review explains the concern or missing context and asks a focused question. Suggested alternatives wait for your explicit acceptance.

↗ Explain → ask → fresh request

You stay in control.

  • ✓One change, one Undo. The complete edit lands together; Undo restores the previous canvas.
  • ✓No stale overwrite. If you change the canvas while the AI works, its pending batch is rejected.
  • ✓No unfinished batch. Failed checks, unresolved repairs, or interruption before commitment leave draft edits unapplied.
  • ✓Fresh context on follow-up. Accepting a proposal starts a new edit against the current canvas.
Editing is not execution

A reviewed plan, not a live test.

The harness edits the workflow definition. Its tools do not run the workflow, browse the target website, or publish changes.

The reviewer is a separate, read-only AI assessment. It checks intent and behavior using the available evidence; it cannot guarantee real-world outcomes or fill in unknown facts.
